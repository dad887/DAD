Plan one dominant foreground subject group that makes the poster feel complete, imaginative, and professionally designed.

Rules:
1. Use short, iconic asset labels rather than a design report.
2. Brainstorm 5-6 short candidate assets, then select only the best 3-4 final assets.
3. The final assets should form one designed poster motif, not a realistic scene and not a stock-object collection.

11. For education/activity themes, prefer one person or character doing something with a theme prop.
